# Artwork sources

Portfolio illustrations are Xinyi Zhang's supplied artwork from *In Seek of Homeland*, all 45 PDF pages. Individual chapters preserve the complete original pages and credits. The design workflow is from *Where the Fire Stopped*, Autodesk submission, PDF page 25. Rendering and cropping retained original visual content.

The home clearing is an original concept illustration made with the built-in Imagegen tool, not a photograph of an actual site or a copy of the Pollinator Pathmaker artwork. The portfolio remains the source for the actual project illustrations and visual accents.

## Generation prompt

Create one refined, fresh Nordic botanical garden illustration of an exploratory clearing, in a wide 3:2 landscape format. Clean very pale ivory paper, nearly white #fbfcf7. An airy oval clearing centered with generous margins and open central breathing room. Irregular botanical edges fade naturally into the page, no terrain slab or boxed boundary. Delicate white-trunk birches, airy foliage, wildflowers, grassy patches, and modest landscape architectural fragments surround the clearing. A subtle walking path connects three patches: a small pale house among trees at upper left; tall trees and meadow at right; an open field notebook and architectural drawings at lower center, with only nonlinguistic pencil sketches. Aerial oblique axonometric view. Delicate hand-painted cutout botanical forms, translucent watercolor washes, refined botanical textures and light pencil detail. Muted fresh leaf greens, sky blue, pale butter yellow, light violet flowers, tiny warm coral accents. Quiet daylight. No words, letters, numbers, labels, UI, people, vehicles, logos, watermarks, photorealism, video-game aesthetics, chunky low-poly plastic, dark outlined cartoon, soil slab, hard frame, or floating island.

## White-background revision

The existing clearing illustration was edited once with built-in Imagegen: preserve the composition and botanical detail; replace beige, cream, ivory, and off-white background washes with flat pure white #FFFFFF; remove paper texture; retain cool foliage, fine lines, and restrained flowers; render the path with subtle cool gray contours on white.

## Human-scale clearing revision

One Imagegen edit retained white paper and botanical linework while moving to an oblique view along a receding garden path. Cropped foreground birches and flowers, a human-scale drawing table, two walking figures, and a distant house establish depth and scale.

## Display typography

Clearing Display is original uppercase lettering generated by `scripts/build-display-font.py`. It uses a narrow vertical and broad horizontal pen to explore the reverse-contrast character of the user’s GSAPP title reference. No GSAPP font outlines or font files are included. Source and generated TTF/WOFF2 are supplied with the site.
