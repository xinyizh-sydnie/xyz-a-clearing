# Artwork sources

Portfolio illustrations are Xinyi Zhang's supplied artwork from *In Seek of Homeland*, all 45 PDF pages. Individual chapters preserve the complete original pages and credits. The design workflow is from *Where the Fire Stopped*, Autodesk submission, PDF page 25. Rendering and cropping retained original visual content.

The home clearing is an original concept illustration made with the built-in Imagegen tool, not a photograph of an actual site or a copy of the Pollinator Pathmaker artwork. The portfolio remains the source for the actual project illustrations and visual accents.

## Generation prompt

Create one refined, fresh Nordic botanical garden illustration of an exploratory clearing, in a wide 3:2 landscape format. Clean very pale ivory paper, nearly white #fbfcf7. An airy oval clearing centered with generous margins and open central breathing room. Irregular botanical edges fade naturally into the page, no terrain slab or boxed boundary. Delicate white-trunk birches, airy foliage, wildflowers, grassy patches, and modest landscape architectural fragments surround the clearing. A subtle walking path connects three patches: a small pale house among trees at upper left; tall trees and meadow at right; an open field notebook and architectural drawings at lower center, with only nonlinguistic pencil sketches. Aerial oblique axonometric view. Delicate hand-painted cutout botanical forms, translucent watercolor washes, refined botanical textures and light pencil detail. Muted fresh leaf greens, sky blue, pale butter yellow, light violet flowers, tiny warm coral accents. Quiet daylight. No words, letters, numbers, labels, UI, people, vehicles, logos, watermarks, photorealism, video-game aesthetics, chunky low-poly plastic, dark outlined cartoon, soil slab, hard frame, or floating island.

## White-background revision

The existing clearing illustration was edited once with built-in Imagegen: preserve the composition and botanical detail; replace beige, cream, ivory, and off-white background washes with flat pure white #FFFFFF; remove paper texture; retain cool foliage, fine lines, and restrained flowers; render the path with subtle cool gray contours on white.

## Human-scale clearing revision

One Imagegen edit retained white paper and botanical linework while moving to an oblique view along a receding garden path. Cropped foreground birches and flowers, a human-scale drawing table, two walking figures, and a distant house establish depth and scale.

## A journey through the projects

Eight new, separately generated Imagegen illustrations form a continuous interpretive walk: Fire at the urban edge, Around the home, Along a seasonal path, A place for young people, Living with water, A place to gather, At the shifting shore, and A path of return. The walk then connects the existing Ant Scape ground study and human-scale clearing. Each new asset was requested once, at a wide 3:2 aspect ratio, using white paper/sky, botanical watercolor and architectural pencil linework, an eye-height viewpoint, foreground detail, and a path receding into the distance. Final PNGs were converted to WebP for delivery.

The scenes are conceptual entrances, not documentation of actual research locations or a claim that the depicted home is fire-safe. The portfolio's original illustrations and project credits remain in the project readers. Visual reference pages were 8–9 (seasonal planting and creek), 14 (youth district), 20–21 (water settlement), 24–25 (community market), 29 (sediment structures), and 41–42 (reconstructed Japanese town and silk-street gardens). Fire and home scenes interpret the research themes in the same illustration language.

Companion text uses the open-source Jost variable font, from the official Google Fonts `ofl/jost` distribution, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The font and its license are included in `public/fonts/`. Jost complements the existing original Clearing Display lettering; no GSAPP font file is distributed.

## Shared clearing overview

A single new Imagegen composition connects the individual project scenes into a shared central clearing, visible at both the beginning and end of the walk. Continuous paths and water link a distant fire ridge, house garden, seasonal creek, campus commons, floating planted beds, market arcades, sediment edge, reconstructed garden lane, a drawing table, and the ant trail. Five existing scene illustrations were supplied as reference images. This is an interpretive landscape, not a claim that the separate projects occupy one actual geographical site. The original generated PNG was converted to `public/data/exploration/scenes/clearing-hub.webp`. No variants or retries were generated.

## Repository data layout

Media are grouped by purpose under `public/data/exploration/`, `public/data/portfolio/`, and `public/data/research/`. Source text, metadata and spatial links are under `data/`. `data/assets.json` indexes all media files with byte lengths and SHA-256 checksums. The directory migration preserves the supplied image and PDF bytes.

## Immersive viewing

The immersive walk reuses the existing scene paintings unchanged. At runtime the painting is composited into soft foreground and distance bands; these respond at different rates to pointer movement. This is an illustrative parallax treatment, not a reconstructed 3D environment. The original source images and their provenance above remain unchanged.
